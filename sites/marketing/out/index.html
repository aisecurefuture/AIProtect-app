What it does

Safe Links

Checks a link before the page opens.

Paste a link, or let the browser extension check it as you click. You get one of three answers — safe, be careful, or blocked — with a plain sentence saying why, and a list of what we actually checked.

Privacy Guard

Warns you before you share personal information with an AI.

On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others, we read the text in the box at the moment you press send and tell you if it contains passwords, keys or personal details. You can always send it anyway.

AI Safety

Spots pages that try to hijack an AI assistant.

Some web pages hide instructions aimed at the AI reading them, to make it leak information or misbehave. We flag those before they reach your assistant.

What we can and can’t see

What we look at

  • · Links you check, or click with the extension installed.
  • · Text you’re about to send to an AI assistant, at the moment you send it.
  • · Only on the handful of AI sites we list — nowhere else on the web.

What we don’t do

  • · We don’t open your documents. No PDFs, spreadsheets or photos.
  • · We don’t store the text you check.
  • · We’re not antivirus, and we don’t scan your files.
  • · We don’t read your email or your messages.
